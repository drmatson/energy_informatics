# ⚡ Energy Informatics — Data Science for the Digital Energy Sector

📚 **Course website:** https://drmatson.github.io/energy_informatics/ 

This repository contains materials for the course **“Data Science for the Digital Energy Sector”**, focused on how data science enables the digital transformation of the energy sector—from data cleaning and feature engineering to time-series forecasting and decision-making with optimization. ⚡📈🧠

A strong emphasis is placed on **time series analysis** (statistical + machine learning) and practical energy datasets (prices, demand, PV, etc.). The course culminates in **optimal control** concepts for energy management, showing how forecasts translate into actionable decisions (e.g., storage scheduling). 🔋✅

---

## 🧭 Course roadmap (modules)

### 1) 🐍 Python / NumPy / Pandas
- Introduction to Python
- NumPy
- Pandas

### 2) 🔁 Data Science Lifecycle
- From problem framing to evaluation and communication

### 3) 📊 Visualisation
- Matplotlib & Seaborn
- ⚡ Plotly
- ⚙️ Interactive controls in Jupyter (ipywidgets)
- ⚡ Dash for Energy Analytics (mini tutorial)

### 4) 🧹 Data preprocessing
- Handling datetime
- Handling missing data
- Outliers in data
- Feature engineering tutorial
- Principal Component Analysis (PCA)

### 5) ⏱️ Statistical modelling for time series
- Time series vs timestamped data
- Trend/seasonality/decomposition
- ACF/PACF, SARIMA

### 6) 🤖 Machine learning for time series
- ⚡ Baselines (Lag-7 day, Linear Regression)
- Random Forest
- SVM/SVR
- Prophet
- ⏱️ Chronos (time-series forecasting)
- 🧠 TimesFM (foundation model for time series)

### 7) 🧩 Forecasting strategies
- Practical strategies for training, validation, and deployment

### 8) 🏠⚡ Home Energy Management Systems (HEMS)
- What is a Home EMS and why it matters
- Optimization-based energy scheduling (incl. cost minimization)
- ⚙️ Pyomo-based modeling concepts (decision variables, objective, constraints)

---

## 🎯 Learning outcomes
By the end of the course, you should be able to:
- 🧼 Clean and structure energy time series (timestamps, missing values, outliers)
- 🧰 Engineer features for energy forecasting (calendar, weather/price signals where applicable)
- 📉 Build and evaluate forecasting models using strong baselines + ML approaches
- 📊 Visualize results and communicate performance and uncertainty clearly
- 🔋 Formulate a simple energy management optimization problem and interpret results
